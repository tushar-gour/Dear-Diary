# Diary

### A quiet place for your everyday.

Little moments. Big feelings. Everything in between.

**Android · Offline · No online app account**

Diary is a personal diary for your thoughts, memories and everyday moments. A calm space to write, save and look back, at your own pace.

### Make it yours

- **Personal books:** choose gentle covers and title designs for different chapters of your life.
- **Pages your way:** write, record voice notes and add selected photos or songs.
- **Find a moment:** revisit pages with local search, favorites and Memories.
- **Your choice of protection:** optional app and book locks, with encrypted backups you choose to create.

### Your data, your control

Diary does not require an online app account. Read the policy for how your information is handled, and the deletion guide for removing your data directly on your device.
